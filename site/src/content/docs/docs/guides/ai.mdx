:::tip[AI is optional and off until you turn it on]
Nothing else in the app depends on it. Until you switch it on (the **AI explanations** card on
Home, or **Settings → AI explanations**) there is no ✦ button anywhere and nothing is sent to any AI
service. Switch it off again any time.
:::

## Turning it on

On **Home**, switch on **AI explanations**. The card then shows what the ✦ button will do and the
one step left, if any: in *AI app* mode it's ready when Gemini (or another AI app) is installed; in
*API key* mode, **Get a free Gemini key** opens Google AI Studio and **AI settings** takes you
where the key goes.

The lookup popup has an AI button (✦) next to **+ Anki**. By default it asks for a short
grammar breakdown of the sentence in English, focused on how the word you tapped is used: its
form, the patterns around it, and a natural translation.

## Two ways to answer

**AI app (no setup, free).** The button opens an AI app you already have, with the prompt typed
in. Gemini puts it straight into its message box: tap send. Claude, ChatGPT and other apps get
it through the share sheet, and the prompt is also copied, so you can paste it if the app doesn't fill it in.
Pick the app under **Settings → AI explanations → Answers → AI app**.

**API key (answers in the popup).** The answer streams into the popup in place of the
definition. Tap ✦ again or press Back to return to the definition. Chips switch between prompts,
and ↻ asks again. Pick a provider under **Answers → API key**, paste your key, pick a model from
the list and tap **Send a test**.

### Getting a free key (no credit card)

Checked on 2026-11-20 with brand-new accounts:

| Provider | Key | What works for free |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Gemini** | [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey): sign in with a Google account | The default model, `gemini-flash-lite-latest`, answered in under 1 s. The bigger Flash models sometimes answer "busy" on the free tier: try again or keep Flash-Lite. |
| **OpenRouter** | [openrouter.ai/settings/keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) | Models ending in `:free` (listed first) and the default `openrouter/free`, which picks a free model for you. Free models are rate-limited; if one is busy, pick another. |
| **Ollama Cloud** | [ollama.com/settings/keys](https://ollama.com/settings/keys) | The key is free and lists the models, but answers asked for credits or a plan. |

Both Gemini and OpenRouter work right after you paste the key: the model is already filled in.

### Other providers

| Provider | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Groq, Cerebras, Mistral | Free tiers (not checked here). |
| OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, xAI | Paid. |
| OpenCode Go | Subscription; open models such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3. |
| Ollama (local) | Your own computer on the same network, no key: enter its address. |
| Custom endpoint | Any API that speaks the OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini format. |

Keys are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore and only sent to the provider you picked.

## Step by step: free answers in the popup

1. Switch on **AI explanations** on Home (or in **Settings → AI explanations**) and choose **API key**.
2. Pick **Google Gemini** (or **OpenRouter**) as the provider.
3. Tap **Get a key from Google Gemini**, sign in, create a key and copy it.
4. Paste it into **API key**. The model is already filled in (`gemini-flash-lite-latest`).
5. Tap **Send a test**: *Answered in 1.0 s* means you're done.
6. Look up any word and tap ✦ in the popup.

## Models change; the app keeps up

Model names come from the provider's live list, not from the app, so new models show up without
an update. Defaults are aliases where the provider has them (`gemini-flash-lite-latest`,
`openrouter/free`). If a model you picked is retired or renamed, the next answer switches to the
best model the provider still lists, says so above the answer (*… isn't available anymore:
switched to …*) and saves the new choice.

## When something goes wrong

The popup says what failed and what to do, with only the buttons that help:

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *No internet connection* | Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then **Try again**. |
| *… refused your API key* | The key was deleted or mistyped: **Open AI settings** and paste a new one. |
| *This model needs credits* | Pick a free model (OpenRouter: `:free`), or add credits with the provider. |
| *… limit is used up for now* | Free tiers allow a few requests a minute: wait, or pick another model. |
| *… is busy right now* / *took too long* | The model is overloaded: **Try again** in a moment, or pick a lighter model. |
| *This model isn't available anymore* | No replacement was found: pick a model in settings. |

**Ask Gemini** under every answer is the way out that always works: it opens the Gemini app with
the same prompt. **Send a test** in settings shows the same messages, so you can fix a key or model
before you need it.

## Prompts

**Settings → AI explanations → Prompts** lists the prompts the popup offers: *Grammar* (the
default), *Translate* and *Word nuance*, plus any you add. Tap one to edit it. Placeholders are
filled from the lookup: `{word}`, `{sentence}`, `{lemma}` (dictionary form), `{reading}`,
`{definition}` and `{language}`. Tap a placeholder chip to insert it at the cursor.

Built-in prompts can't be lost: an edited one shows **Edited by you**, with **Restore original** to
go back to the text that shipped. **Run first** makes a prompt the one the button starts with.

## On the card

An answer you opened in the popup can go on the card you mine next:

- **After the glossary** (default) or **Before the glossary**: in the same field as the definitions.
- **In its own field**: a field named *Grammar* or *AI Explanation*, or any field you set to
  *AI explanation* in **Settings → Anki → Fields**. To use it with an existing note type (Anime
  Cards, Lapis), add a *Grammar* field in AnkiDroid once.
- **Not added**.

Mining never calls the AI on its own: only an answer you already read goes on the card.
