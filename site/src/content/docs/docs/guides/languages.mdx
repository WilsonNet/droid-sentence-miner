title: Languages
description: Study Japanese (the main focus), English or German. Only the language you pick downloads dictionaries and OCR models.

Droid Sentence Miner is built for Japanese first, and it supports other languages as small
modules. On first launch it asks **What are you learning?**: Japanese is preselected. Change it
any time in **Settings → Language**.

## What follows the language

- **Lookups** use only that language's dictionaries. Dictionaries of the other language stay
  installed but are hidden and unused, so you can switch back without reinstalling.
- **Settings → Dictionaries** shows that language's dictionaries and one-tap recommendations.
- **OCR clean-up** keeps the language's spacing and punctuation (English keeps spaces and `!?`,
  Japanese uses full-width punctuation and joins wrapped lines without spaces).
- **Downloads**: nothing for a language you don't study. The APK carries only a few KB of code
  per language.

## English

- **Dictionaries**: install *Wiktionary English* (English → English, about 110 MB download) and
  *Wiktionary English IPA* (pronunciations, 3 MB) under **Settings → Dictionaries**. Small
  English → Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean
  glossaries are there too.
- **Words and phrases**: tap a word and the longest dictionary match wins, up to five words, so
  *looked up*, *ice cream* and *give up on* open as phrases. Inflections are undone with parts of
  speech: *running → run*, *happiest → happy*, *wolves → wolf*, *looked up → look up*.
- **Pronunciation**: the IPA is shown under the headword and goes into the card's *IPA* field
  (or *Reading*, on a note type without one). Word audio comes from Wiktionary's recordings on Wikimedia Commons (US first).
- **OCR**: without downloads, English is read by the built-in ML Kit model. For games and
  subtitles install the **English OCR models** (23 MB) in **Settings → Language**: PaddleOCR made
  about a third of ML Kit's word errors in our test (3.5 % vs 12.2 %) and is faster.

## German

- **Dictionaries**: install *Wiktionary German → English* (17 MB) and *Wiktionary German IPA*
  (1.5 MB). *Wiktionary German* (German → German, 55 MB) and German → Portuguese, Spanish, French,
  Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean glossaries are there too. Which is which:
  [dictionary list](https://github.com/WilsonNet/droid-sentence-miner/blob/main/docs/dictionaries.md).
- **Words and phrases**: whole words and phrases of up to four words, hyphenated compounds
  (*E-Mail*). Nouns keep their capital; a capitalized word at the start of a sentence and ALL-CAPS
  game text are looked up too. Wiktionary lists most inflected forms itself (*ging → gehen*,
  *Häuser → Haus*); regular ones are undone on top: *gemacht → machen*, *aufgemacht → aufmachen*,
  *anzufangen → anfangen*, *schnelleren → schnell*, *Lehrerinnen → Lehrerin*. Separated prefixes
  (*Ich stehe früh auf*) aren't joined yet.
- **Cards**: the *Gender* field gets the noun's article (*der Hund*, *die Katze*), the *IPA* field
  the pronunciation. Word audio: Wiktionary's recordings (standard German first, then Austrian and
  Swiss), then Lingua Libre.
- **OCR**: the built-in ML Kit model reads umlauts and ß; the optional OCR models (23 MB, the same
  PaddleOCR pack as English) work too. Hyphenated line breaks (*Wör-* / *terbuch*) are joined.

## Japanese

Everything else on this site describes Japanese: game-trained meikiocr, pitch accent, Jitendex,
JapanesePod101 word audio, vertical book pages.

## More languages

A language is a small Kotlin module plus a list of recommended dictionaries and, optionally, a
downloadable OCR pack. If you want to bring your language, see the
[contributor guide](https://github.com/WilsonNet/droid-sentence-miner/blob/main/.opencode/skills/dsm-language/SKILL.md).
