title: Books and manga
description: Photograph a Japanese page, read tategaki and horizontal print, and tap the printed text to look up words and mine the page.
---

The **Book** tab is for paper: light novels, manga, textbooks and anything else that is not on a
screen. A photo is read with ML Kit, laid out as Japanese page text, and overlaid on the image so
the printed characters themselves become tappable.

## Take a page photo

1. Open the **Book** tab.
2. Tap **Take photo** and photograph the page flat and well lit, filling as much of the frame as
   possible. On Samsung phones this opens Samsung Camera; elsewhere it opens any camera app that
   handles image capture.
3. Wait for the page to be read. Paragraphs are outlined on the photo.

The photo is decoded upright (EXIF is applied) and capped at 3000 px on the long edge. No camera
permission is needed because the camera app returns the image.

You can also pick a photo from the gallery with the image button, or share a photo from another
app to **Droid Sentence Miner**: it opens in the Book tab and is read right away.

## Layout

Book mode is not a generic OCR field. It understands Japanese page layout:

- **Tategaki** columns are ordered right to left, then top to bottom.
- **Horizontal** lines are ordered top to bottom.
- Kana-only ruby lines and very thin lines are dropped. Ruby is detected by width relative to the
  median column, after OCR text fixes.
- Page numbers and non-Japanese blocks are ignored.
- A block that ends mid-sentence is joined with the adjacent block.
- `……` is restored after Unicode normalization (NFKC turns it into dots).

## Mine from a paragraph

1. Tap a paragraph on the photo to open its text.
2. Tap a word to open the definition popup. Tap Japanese inside a definition to look that up too;
   the back arrow returns.
3. Tap **Anki**. The card image is the paragraph cropped from the photo, and the sentence is cut
   at 。！？ while keeping closing quotes.

Hold and drag over the text to force your own selection. A hold without dragging selects one
character, which is useful for a single kanji. The ✎ button corrects OCR text before mining; word
positions are preserved for the parts that did not change.

## Read on the photo

After a photo the page opens in an **overlay reader**:

- the OCR text sits invisibly over the printed characters;
- tap any character to look up the word starting there;
- hold and drag to select characters;
- pinch to zoom and drag to pan;
- ✕ or Back returns to the paragraph view.

This is the closest thing to tapping a real manga page. The page stays alive across tab switches,
so you can leave the Book tab and come back without taking another photo.

## Limitations

Book photos always use ML Kit. meikiocr is a horizontal game-text reader and is not used for
tategaki pages. ML Kit can still misread stylized manga fonts; use ✎ before mining.
